# Hyperscalers, explained

How the companies that rent out the world's computing power make money, what the AI build-out costs, who pays for it, and what has to be true for it to pay off.

**The short answer.** The revenue is real, growing fast and, for the biggest players, backed by years of signed contracts. The bill is growing faster, and more of it now arrives as depreciation (the cost of chips wearing out) and interest. The winners would be the companies whose revenue from each gigawatt of data centers stays ahead of both. On today's numbers that favors the three cloud giants, which have large, profitable older businesses underneath them, and the companies that sell them chips and power equipment. The risk sits mostly with the GPU clouds and builders that borrowed to grow.
